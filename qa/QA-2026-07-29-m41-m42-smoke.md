# QA — M41 + M42 smoke test

**Scope:** M41 (EOY projection, monthly review snapshot, surplus drift, allocation
readiness) and M42 (the `/operations` IA rebuild, health score).
**Environment:** production, `/he` unless a step says otherwise.
**Time:** ~35 minutes.

## How to use this document

Work top to bottom — later sections depend on state the earlier ones create. Each check
has an **expected** result. Where a refusal is expected, **the refusal IS the pass**:
this module deliberately declines to produce numbers it cannot stand behind, and a
confident figure in those places is the failure.

Record results as `✅` / `❌` / `N/A` against the check number, with a one-line note on
anything that surprised you. A `❌` needs the exact on-screen text, not a paraphrase —
several defects this milestone were only diagnosable from the wording.

**Before starting:** press *recompute* once in the Opportunity Center. Two assumption
versions changed across M41 (`projection_min_closed_months`) and pinned recommendations
are invalidated by design.

## Known-good refusals — do NOT report these as bugs

These are the module's design, and each one exists because its opposite shipped as a
defect at some point:

- No FX card at all (rows predate `originalCurrency`)
- EOY refusing under 3 closed months
- The first-ever month close raising no drift alert
- The health score's `goals` component unmeasured
- A renegotiation or timing card showing **no** saving figure
- The mortgage appearing on **neither** the subscriptions nor the renegotiation card
- A drift alert carrying a provisional caveat rather than being withheld

## If something fails

Capture the exact on-screen text and the URL. For anything numeric, also grab the row
from the database — several defects this milestone were correct in the DB and wrong only
in the UI, and the reverse. The two most useful queries:

```sql
SELECT year, month, status, "surplusBase", "surplusIsProvisional", "reviewSnapshotId"
FROM "OperatingPeriod" ORDER BY year, month;

SELECT type, status, count(*) FROM "Recommendation"
WHERE origin = 'OPERATIONAL' GROUP BY type, status ORDER BY type;
```
